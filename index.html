Our Mission

Raise Awareness

We provide a platform for dog-lovers, activists and organizations to share new initiatives and success stories — ensuring productive partnerships and collaborations for the dogs that need it most.

The Data

Crowdsourced Research

The crowdsourced data collected through this app is part of research to gain insight into the secret lives of street dogs.

In later stages, this information will be used to develop strategies that improve their welfare and enable international adoption.

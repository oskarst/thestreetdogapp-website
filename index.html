We want to provide a platform for dog-lovers, activists and organizations to share with each other new initiatives and success stories to ensure productive partnership and collaborations.

Crowd Sourced Data

The crowd sourced data, collected through this app, is a part of AI driven research to get an insight into the secret lifes of the street dogs.

In later stages of project, this information will be used to develop and implement strategies to improve their welfare and international adoption.
